FAQ

Questions, answered.

What type of businesses do you work with?

Coaches, experts, creators and coaching businesses that sell their knowledge, such as business, health, performance and course coaches.

Do you build automation systems?

Yes. We build CRMs, pipelines, funnels, landing pages, forms, calendars, AI voice and conversation agents, and the email, SMS and WhatsApp follow-up that connects them.

Can I order automation without content?

Yes. Use the custom package builder to choose only the systems you want. Custom orders start at $750.

Can I create a custom package?

Yes. Use the custom package builder to pick the exact systems and content you want. The total updates as you add items.

What is the minimum custom order?

Custom orders start at $750.

Are platform costs included?

No. Third-party platform, messaging and AI usage costs are billed separately by those providers where they apply.

Can I add extra services?

Yes. Add-ons such as extra videos, hooks, thumbnails, carousels, landing pages and workflows can be added to any package. All prices are listed on the Pricing page.

Do you edit both short-form and long-form videos?

Yes. We edit Reels and Shorts as well as long-form videos and podcasts, and we can cut short clips from your long-form content.

Do you provide the raw footage?

No. You supply the raw footage, and we edit it. We work with the files you share in your project brief.

How many revisions are included?

Packages include 1 revision round per asset. Extra revisions can be discussed on your strategy call.

How does the process work?

We start with a strategy call, then build your systems, create your content, and review and improve everything every month.

What happens after I choose a package?

You fill in a short project brief with your business details, tool access, references, brand guidelines, files and deadline so we can start.
