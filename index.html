Attention is easy to lose. Follow-up shouldn't be.

Under the hood

Simple rules. Reliable results.

Every automation is a clear trigger and a set of actions you can read, change and trust. Here is one we set up often: the missed-call text back.

  • Built inside your CRM, so you own it
  • Documented, with a walkthrough at handover
  • Third-party platform, messaging and AI usage costs are billed by those providers
Example workflowMissed-call text back
  1. WhenA call to your business number is missed
  2. ThenSend an SMS: "Sorry we missed you, how can we help?"
  3. ThenCreate the contact and add it to the pipeline as New
  4. ThenNotify your team in the unified inbox
  5. IfThey reply, the conversation agent offers a time to book
